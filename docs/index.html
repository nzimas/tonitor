Acousmatic firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

For electroacoustic experimentation, live ambient music and sound exploration. Six layers of sound objects, three generative engines, six free loopers — and a screen made for low vision.

What it is

Not a groovebox

No tempo. No steps. No patterns. No drum kit. Tonitor turns the FM-1 into an instrument for acousmatic and electroacoustic music in the line of the GRM and IRCAM — Parmegiani, Henry, Risset, Stockhausen — and for live ambient music, dark ambient above all. You play textures, drones and gestures, layer them, loop them freely out of time with each other, and shape them while they play.

What it adds

Built for listening

Six layers

Each layer has its own engine, sound, two voices, octave, level, pan and effects. A new note never cuts the tail of the last.

FX · SEL · ENV · LFO · EDIT · GLO

FM

Six-operator FM drawing sound objects after the GRM and IRCAM: bells, spectral drones, noise, impacts, iterations, glissandi, Risset's arpeggio, sub-bass, creaks.

PRESETS · HOME for a new sound

ANIMA

A synthesizer played by a simulated hand: oscillators, FM, grains, noise, filters, folding and a resonator, all moved by its gestures. Your key is the note; the hand moves everything else.

PRESETS · HOME for a new sound

CALIGULA

A restless organism after the LYRA-8: eight cross-modulating oscillators, a hyper-LFO, bursts and surges, and a simulated hand on its plates. Rooted, gnarly, never a siren.

PRESETS · HOME for a new sound

Generative paths

Lean each layer's new sounds towards AMBIENT — ethereal, melodic, dark — or EXPERIMENTAL — edgier, gnarlier, more textured.

The two leftmost black keys

Free loopers

A loop recorder on every layer, up to ten overdubs each, every loop its own length. Start or stop them all at once.

REC · PLAY · SHIFT + PLAY

Latched drones

Hold notes, press SHIFT: they keep sounding, their keys lit, while you play on top.

Hold keys, then SHIFT

Filter, grains, crush

On every layer a resonant DJ filter, a granulator from dark drifting clouds to scattered shards, and a bit and sample-rate crush.

KNOB 1 · KNOB 2 · KNOB 3

Master reverb

A lush plate after Dattorro, fed by a send on every layer.

KNOB 4 · SEQ for the reverb page

Scales

The white keys play a scale from any root: major, minors, dorian, pentatonic, whole tone, enigmatic, the Japanese in.

ARP

Sessions

64 slots in the FM-1's flash: every layer, sound, loop and setting, saved and loaded in a moment.

SAVE

Made for low vision

Very few words, in very large type, with high contrast; each layer in its own colour.

Always on

Install

From your browser

The installer runs in a desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera and talks to the FM-1 over USB MIDI. Nothing to download, nothing uploaded.

  1. Connect the FM-1 to the computer with a USB data cable and switch it on.
  2. Open the installer, read the warning and allow MIDI access.
  3. Press Install and leave the FM-1 connected until it restarts into Tonitor.
