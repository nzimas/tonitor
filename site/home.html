A firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 for electroacoustic experimentation, live ambient music and sound exploration

Install in your browser Read the manual

Features

Six layers
Each with its own engine, sound, two voices, octave, level and pan.
FM
Six-operator FM that draws sound objects: bells, drones, noise, impacts, glissandi, sub-bass.
ANIMA
A synthesizer played by a simulated hand; your key sets the note, the hand moves the rest.
CALIGULA
Eight cross-modulating oscillators that stir, burst and surge while a key is held.
Generative paths
New sounds lean towards ambient (dark, ethereal) or experimental (edgy, textured).
Free loopers
One per layer, up to ten overdubs, each loop its own length.
Latching
Hold notes, press SHIFT, and they sustain while you play on top.
Filter, grains, crush
A resonant filter, a granulator and a bit and rate crush on every layer.
Reverb
A deep plate reverb, with a send from every layer.
Scales
The white keys play a scale from any root.
Sessions
64 slots holding every layer, sound, loop and setting.
Low vision
Few words, very large type, high contrast, a colour per layer.

Install

The installer runs in a desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera and talks to the FM-1 over USB MIDI. Nothing to download, nothing uploaded.

  1. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable and switch it on.
  2. Open the installer, read the warning and allow MIDI access.
  3. Press Install and leave the FM-1 connected until it restarts into Tonitor.

Tonitor is unofficial. It is not made, endorsed or supported by M-VAVE, and installing it may void any warranty that applies to your FM-1. You install it at your own risk; see the disclaimer.
